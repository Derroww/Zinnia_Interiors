The Studio

True lines first, warmth after.

Founded in Nairobi, Zinnia Interiors approaches residential spaces with architectural discipline. We believe structural clarity must precede decoration. By limiting our workflow to eight major residential handovers per year, we ensure total design integrity from the first draft to final handover styling.

Our Kilimani studio houses our design team, material library, and client review boards. It is here that every layout variant is tested, line by line, before construction begins.
