Not just chosen because it “feels right,” but because every piece in it answers a real question about how you live — where the light lands at 4pm, where a coat actually gets dropped, what a table needs to survive a Tuesday. How We Work

Four phases. The same order, every time.

Phase 01

Discovery

We walk the space, measure it properly, and ask questions most designers skip — what time you're home, what you'll actually clean, what you're keeping.

Phase 02

Concept & Drawings

Floor plans, elevations, and a full material palette — a complete set of drawings, reviewed and approved before we source a single item.

Phase 03

Sourcing & Specification

Every material, fixture, and piece of furniture is selected against the drawings, scheduled, and ordered with lead times mapped out.

Phase 04

Installation

The reveal: furniture placed, art hung, every last object styled in a single day. You leave in the morning and come home to the finished room.
