Our Philosophy

We take on eight residential projects a year, start to finish, and hand you a full set of drawings before a single thing is bought.

Founded in Nairobi — Operating across Nairobi County and its environs

Structure first, warmth after.

At Zinnia Interiors, we approach interior architecture with a meticulous focus on technical design variables. We don't believe in immediate decoration. Instead, every structural blueprint, partition detail, elevation line, and electrical asset schematic is completely locked in place before a single piece of fabric or item of loose furniture is ordered.

By intentionally limiting our scope of operation to a tight roster of client handovers per year, our studio provides hyper-focused contract administration, site management, and styling overlays. This ensures that what is signed off on paper is exactly what gets built inside your home.
